Privacy Policy - Agrikranti 1928

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · App: Agrikranti 1928 (web app and Android app) · Developer: Aditya Kumar

This policy explains what information the Agrikranti 1928 app collects, why, and how you can delete it. यह नीति बताती है कि ऐप कौन सी जानकारी लेता है, क्यों, और आप उसे कैसे हटा सकते हैं।

1. Information we collect / हम कौन सी जानकारी लेते हैं

  • Account details: name, mobile number and (if you use OTP login) e-mail address, used to log you in and keep your results and purchases linked to you.
  • Test activity: test scores, answers and time taken, stored on your phone and, if online features are on, on our server to show results and leaderboards.
  • Purchases (website only): when you buy an eBook on our website, payment is processed by Razorpay / UPI apps. We receive only the payment ID and status, never your card, bank or UPI PIN details.
  • Ask AI questions: questions you type in "Ask AI" are sent to Google Gemini API to generate an answer. Do not type personal or sensitive information there.
  • Anonymous usage counts: number of app opens, installs and tests submitted, counted without your name or number.

2. How we use it / उपयोग

  • To provide tests, results, papers and study material.
  • To verify login and prevent misuse of one account on many phones.
  • To improve the app. We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

3. Sharing / साझा करना

Data is shared only with service providers needed to run the app: Google (Apps Script, Gemini API, hosting on GitHub Pages by GitHub), Razorpay for website payments, and a counter service for anonymous counts. They process data as per their own privacy policies.

4. Storage and security / सुरक्षा

Most data stays on your phone (browser storage). Server data is kept in a Google Sheet accessible only to the developer and is deleted on request. Connections use HTTPS.

5. Children

The app is meant for students preparing for agriculture exams (usually 16+). It is not directed at children under 13.

6. Delete your account and data / डेटा हटाना

Open the app → Profile → Account delete करें, or follow the steps on the Account Deletion page. Local data is removed immediately and server data is deleted within 7 days of your request.

7. Contact / संपर्क

Telegram: @Agrikranti_Aditya_Kumar · YouTube: Agrikranti 1928
