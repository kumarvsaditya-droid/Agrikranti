Cancellation and Refund Policy - Agrikranti 1928

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Agrikranti 1928, run by Aditya Kumar, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India

Our paid products are digital eBooks that are delivered instantly inside the app. हमारे पेड प्रोडक्ट डिजिटल ई-बुक हैं जो तुरंत ऐप में मिल जाती हैं।

1. Cancellation

As the eBook is unlocked immediately after payment, an order cannot be cancelled once the payment is successful.

2. When you get a full refund / कब पूरा पैसा वापस मिलेगा

  • Money was debited but the eBook did not unlock and we could not unlock it within 48 hours of your complaint.
  • You were charged twice for the same eBook (duplicate payment).
  • Wrong amount was charged due to a technical error.

3. No refund

  • After the eBook has been unlocked and opened on your account.
  • Change of mind, or if the content is not what you expected after reading the free sample. Please read the free sample before buying.

4. How to request

Send your name, registered mobile number, eBook name and payment ID / UPI reference to us on Contact Us within 7 days of payment.

5. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5 - 7 working days to the original payment method (bank account / UPI / card). स्वीकृत रिफंड 5 - 7 कार्य दिवस में उसी खाते में भेजा जाता है।
