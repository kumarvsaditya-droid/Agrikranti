Delete your Agrikranti account / अपना account हटाएँ

App: Agrikranti 1928 · Developer: Aditya Kumar

Option 1 - In the app / ऐप में

Open the app → Profile → Account delete करें → confirm. All data on your phone is removed at once and a deletion request is sent to us.

Option 2 - Without the app / बिना ऐप के

Send a message on Telegram to @Agrikranti_Aditya_Kumar with the text "Delete my Agrikranti account", your name and the registered mobile number.

What is deleted / क्या हटेगा

Your name, mobile number, e-mail, test results and login records are deleted from our server within 7 days. Payment IDs of eBook purchases may be kept up to the period required by tax laws, after which they are deleted. Anonymous usage counts do not contain your identity.
