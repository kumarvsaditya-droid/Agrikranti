Shipping and Delivery Policy - Agrikranti 1928

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Agrikranti 1928, run by Aditya Kumar, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India

Agrikranti sells only digital products. No physical item is shipped. हम केवल डिजिटल प्रोडक्ट देते हैं, कोई सामान डाक से नहीं भेजा जाता।

1. Delivery method

After successful payment, the purchased eBook is unlocked inside the Agrikranti app / website for the mobile number used at the time of purchase. You can read it from the Downloads / eBooks section.

2. Delivery time

  • Online payment: instant - usually within a few seconds of payment.
  • If the eBook does not open automatically, it is unlocked manually within 24 hours after you contact us with your payment details.

3. Access

The eBook stays available in the app on your device. If you change your phone, contact us with your mobile number and payment ID to restore access.

4. Delivery area

Available to users in India through the internet. Shipping charges: nil.

5. Help

See Contact Us.
